[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain). No attribution is required; it is given here anyway.

## Trees

The scanned leaves of each tree were rendered into a 2×2 leaf-cluster atlas and replaced by crossed cards. The trunk and branches were decimated,
the textures resized to 512 px WebP and the geometry compressed with meshopt.

- `polyhaven/island_tree_02.glb`: Island Tree 02 (https://polyhaven.com/a/island_tree_02), CC0
- `polyhaven/tree_small_02.glb`: Tree Small 02 (https://polyhaven.com/a/tree_small_02), CC0
