# Credits: SimReady 3D assets

The `.glb` models in this folder were made from SimReady assets published on Hugging Face under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) licence (CC BY 4.0).

Changes made to every asset: converted from OpenUSD (or, for the imagine.io plants, from the glTF the same dataset publishes) to glTF binary,
rescaled to metres with Y up, origin moved to the bottom centre, meshes decimated to at most about 60k triangles,
textures resized to 1024 px and re-encoded as WebP, and geometry compressed with meshopt.
The originals carry no endorsement of this project by their authors.
